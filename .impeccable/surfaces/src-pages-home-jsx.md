# Home (/) — surface brief

Scope: the home page. Mode: Persuade. Audience: US families (English and Spanish) planning a party who want a custom 3-arch backdrop. Action: start a custom design (/custom-design) or talk on WhatsApp. Proof: the real set at true scale on its gold stands, wearing real category artwork. Constraints: balloons always labelled not included; stands are gold; no invented prices, reviews or shipping claims; fully bilingual.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page shows the product being assembled — gold frames, then the customer's covers, then an optional balloon idea — on one fixed set, instead of a split hero over a row of category cards.

OWN-WORLD: The custom-design page's world: blush wall over a warm floor, gold tube frames on T-feet, printed fabric covers in arch shapes, Cormorant display with Montserrat text, sage for WhatsApp, blush for the primary action. No kickers above headings.

STORY: The visitor sees what they actually buy (frames + covers), at true scale, tries a theme on the set, understands balloons are only an idea, and starts their own design or messages the business.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left ~40%: display headline, one-line offer, primary "Design yours" and WhatsApp actions, a three-step layer control (Frames · Covers · Balloon idea). Right ~60%: the full set on its stands on a floor, frames visible, centre cover on; theme chips under the set swap the artwork.

FORM: Grounded structure 3 of 7, "the set comes together" — scroll (desktop) or tap (phone) adds one registered layer to the same set; raised by the woodblock challenger's layer-by-layer discipline. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Memorable moment: the covers slide onto the bare gold frames while you scroll, and a theme chip re-dresses the same set instantly.

Unresolved: real customer photos could replace the AI category scenes later.
